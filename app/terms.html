Terms of service

Beta · last updated 7 August 2026

Your images

Your images stay yours. By uploading a photo you grant us a licence to store and process it in order to run the service — removing backgrounds, correcting lighting, and serving your results back to you — and to improve our cutout accuracy over time.

We never use your images to train generative AI. We never sell or license them to anyone. You can delete them at any time. Corrections you make to a cutout are kept as a downscaled copy of the image plus its mask, used solely to make background removal better for painted miniatures.

Processing

Background removal runs on a third-party image-segmentation service, fal.ai, which processes your photo on our behalf as a sub-processor and does not retain it beyond processing. Lighting correction runs on your own device; your original photo is never modified.

Your account

You can use Studioshot without an account. If you sign in to keep your history, we store your uploads and results against your account until you delete them or the account.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: hello@studioshot.app.
